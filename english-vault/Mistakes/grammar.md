# Lỗi hay gặp · grammar (83)

### I need check the email → **I need to check my email.**
- Use the pattern: need to + verb.
- Lặp 14 lần · thành thạo 0%

### After finish working → **After I finish work / After finishing work**
- 'After' followed by a clause needs subject+verb (After I finish work) or a gerund (After finishing work), not the base verb.
- Lặp 2 lần · thành thạo 15%

### to works → **to work**
- After 'to' (infinitive), use the base form of the verb, not the -s form.
- Lặp 2 lần · thành thạo 15%

### have → **has**

### Him → **His**
